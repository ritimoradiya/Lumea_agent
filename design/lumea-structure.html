Why a simulator

Runs through the identical handleInbound() call a real SMS would, stored under the simulator channel. Anyone can try the texting experience in one click — no phone number, no app install.

For an interview that matters: a recruiter clicks a link and is texting your agent in three seconds.

Labelled as a simulator in the UI. It demonstrates the flow, it isn't real SMS. Presenting it as live texting would be dishonest, and a technical interviewer would catch it in one question.
